## 📊 BlinkIT Grocery Sales Analysis & Interactive Excel Dashboard

# Project Summary

This project presents a comprehensive sales analysis of BlinkIT grocery data using Microsoft Excel. The goal is to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights through data cleaning, KPI development, and an interactive dashboard.

The analysis focuses on evaluating product performance, outlet efficiency, customer satisfaction, and geographical sales trends to support data-driven decision-making.

# 🔍 Major Insights
🏬 Outlet Analysis

Supermarket Type 1 accounts for the highest revenue share, indicating better scalability and customer footfall.

🛒 Product Performance

Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods lead total sales, highlighting demand for daily essentials and convenience products.

🥗 Consumer Preference

Low-fat items outperform regular-fat products, reflecting increasing health awareness.

🌍 Location Trends

Tier 3 cities show the highest sales contribution, presenting growth opportunities in semi-urban markets.

⭐ Customer Experience

Consistent average rating near 4 indicates strong service quality and product satisfaction.
